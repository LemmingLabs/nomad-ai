## Outcome

Travelers can compare current flight and accommodation offers for supported searches and continue to the supplier, while businesses can participate in newly enabled markets under a country-specific onboarding and moderation process.

## Why this is a later phase

Live prices and inventory depend on production agreements, market coverage, quotas, and supplier terms. Partner expansion also requires local support and moderation. Research must confirm that the service can lawfully and reliably show the results before implementation begins.

## Completion means

- A provider is approved for a defined set of markets and exposes suitable production search results and redirect links.
- Price and availability results are timestamped, show currency and material conditions, and are refreshed or clearly marked stale.
- The first release is read-only and redirects booking to the supplier; NomadAI does not take payment or issue tickets.
- Business onboarding, moderation, sponsored matching, currency/contact expectations, and analytics can be enabled per country.

## Guardrails

- Do not call an offer a discount without a provider-supplied comparable reference price and conditions.
- Never show cached provider results as current after they expire.
- Do not promise all-market or lowest-price coverage.
- Booking, payment handling, refunds, and travel-agency responsibilities require a separate decision and review.
