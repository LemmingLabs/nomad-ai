## Outcome

An itinerary explains how a traveler spends a day, gives realistic durations and transitions, and communicates where each factual recommendation came from and what remains unverified.

## Why this matters

A location is not a full day plan. A useful itinerary should say what to do at a stop, how long to allow, how to get to the next stop, and which practical facts require verification. AI output and third-party listings are suggestions until supported by evidence.

## Completion means

- Day and stop data describe activities, durations, timing, and practical details while old trips still render.
- Route/time validation catches unrealistic schedules and communicates uncertainty.
- Provider or model failures cannot silently appear as successful real recommendations.
- Route source rights, source dates, attribution, and review state are stored and shown where required.

## Guardrails

- Never copy guide itineraries or scraped pages without usage rights.
- Never mark a route guide-verified without documented review evidence.
- Do not fabricate opening hours, trail access, discounts, or safety advice to fill a missing field.
- Treat sponsored content as a separate commercial source, not as verification.
