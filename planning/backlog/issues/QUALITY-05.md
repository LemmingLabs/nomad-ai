## Context

The shared business/admin dashboard shell always renders a fixed-width sidebar beside the page and has no small-screen navigation treatment. Data tables and forms may have limited space on narrow screens.

## Goal

Make business and admin navigation and the most-used workflows usable on phone, tablet, and desktop widths.

## Acceptance criteria

- Navigation remains reachable and understandable at narrow viewports without consuming most of the content area.
- Dashboard cards, sponsored place forms/media controls, and moderation tables remain readable and operable at supported widths.
- Horizontal overflow is intentional and contained where a table needs it.
- Active navigation, keyboard focus, and role context remain clear.
- Desktop navigation and existing business/admin permissions continue to work.

## Verification

Review business and admin dashboard, sponsored place create/edit, and moderation flows at phone, tablet, and desktop viewport sizes.
