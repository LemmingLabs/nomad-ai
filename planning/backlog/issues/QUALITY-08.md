## Context

The itinerary view contains activities, route information, practical details, provenance, and commercial placements. These distinctions need to remain easy to scan as itinerary detail grows.

## Goal

Help travelers quickly understand each day's plan and distinguish suggestions, estimates, unknown details, and sponsored content.

## Acceptance criteria

- Day headings and activity sequence are visually scannable.
- Duration, time window, travel transition, and practical notes are grouped consistently when present.
- Unknown or estimated values are labeled honestly and are not styled as confirmed facts.
- AI suggestions, catalog/provider facts, route estimates, and sponsored placements remain distinguishable.
- Layout remains readable on narrow screens and with long activity names.

## Verification

Review a complete itinerary, a degraded itinerary, missing-duration details, route-unavailable state, and an itinerary with sponsored content on desktop and mobile.
